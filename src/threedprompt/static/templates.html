Parametric parts from rules, no AI: Gridfinity bins, miniature storage, terrain tiles, gears, lithophanes and QR tags.

Gridfinity bin

Bins are 0.5 mm smaller than the grid so they drop into a baseplate. Magnet holes take 6 x 2 mm magnets.

Miniatures bin (Gridfinity)

One socket per round base, so figures can't slide into each other. Pick the height from your tallest figure (8 units leaves about 45 mm). A 5 x 2 mm magnet takes a 5.2 mm hole.

Terrain tile

152.4 mm = 6 in. Edges always end at the base height, so tiles from any seed join. Terraces give flat steps for miniatures to stand on; the report shows how much of the tile is flat enough.

Involute gears

Gears only mesh with the same module and pressure angle. The mate and rack are placed in mesh.

Lithophane

Print upright at 100 % infill. Darker = thicker, so it looks right when lit from behind.

QR tag

Print the code in a dark colour on a light plate (two colours, or a filament change at the code's first layer).
